## 💡 Key Findings & Insights
* **Margin Leakage:** High sales volume in specific categories (e.g., Office Supplies) does not translate to profit due to aggressive discounting (>20% average).
* **Geographic Profit Drivers:** North America and Western Europe drive over 60% of net profits, whereas emerging markets show high revenue growth but squeezed margins due to high shipping costs.
* **Customer Segment Value:** Corporate accounts generate the highest Average Order Value (AOV) and demonstrate lower sensitivity to shipping fees compared to individual Consumer segments.
